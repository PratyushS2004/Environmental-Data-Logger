Where a number was derived afterward, the entry says so.

## 4. Logic analyzer evidence on the full-log boot (689b536)

The capture file and the full decoder export were lost. What remains is what was pasted or read from screenshots.

Channel mapping as read from the waveform and decoder, not traced on the wires: D0 is CS, D1 is MISO, D2 is SCK, D3 is MOSI. The ground connection was not recorded, and the earlier claim that the pins are PB6, PA5, PA6 and PA7 was never checked.

Decoder counts, from Notepad++ searches on the exported text: 1024 lines contain `MOSI transfers:`, 1023 of them start with `03`, and the one that does not is the first transaction (see below). No line starting `06`, `20` or `02` was found, so no write enable, sector erase or page program appears in the decoded capture. A transaction the decoder dropped would also be invisible, but the total count matches the expected 1024.

Last transaction (slot 0x3FF), decoder text as pasted:

```
8434547-8437257 SPI: MOSI transfers: 03 7F CF F0 FE 03 00 00 BC 83 01 00 89 08 B2 12 CC 2D FD C4
8434547-8437257 SPI: MISO transfers: FF FF FF FF FF 03 00 00 B8 83 01 00 89 08 AA 12 61 A8 46 05
```

Decode of the 16 payload bytes: seq 0x000003FF, pressure 0x000183B8 (99256 Pa), temperature 0x0889 (21.85 C), humidity 0x12AA (47.78 %RH), stored CRC 0x0546A861. The values match the `SAMPLE seq=0x000003FF` line, and address 7F CF F0 returned the record with seq 0x3FF. The 16 MOSI bytes after the header are the previous record (seq 0x3FE), which is leftover receive-buffer content that `HAL_SPI_Receive` transmits.

First two transactions, decoder text as pasted:

```
5430616-5433336 SPI: MOSI transfers: 81 BF C8 00 03 80 00 14 30 7F 00 90 5B 9F 00 04 00 00 00 00
5430616-5433336 SPI: MISO transfers: FF FF FF FF 80 00 00 00 59 C1 80 80 35 84 3B 89 7C 62 AD C5
5433560-5436273 SPI: MOSI transfers: 03 7F 90 10 00 00 00 00 B3 83 01 00 6B 08 77 12 F8 C5 5B 8B
```

The first transaction is the intended `03 7F 90 00` read with one extra leading 1 bit in the decoder's view, which regroups into `81 BF C8 00`. Dropping that bit from the MISO line gives the slot 0 record, whose final byte `8B` is supplied by the second transaction's MOSI leftovers: seq 0, pressure 0x000183B3 (99251 Pa), temperature 0x086B (21.55 C), humidity 0x1277 (47.27 %RH), stored CRC 0x8B5BC5F8. The CRC-32/MPEG-2 of the words 0x00000000, 0x000183B3 and 0x1277086B was recomputed independently and equals 0x8B5BC5F8. The same routine gives 0x0145B905 for the SEEDED header, matching the board anchor.

First-transaction anomaly, observed only: at the sample level, CS falls and SCK has a short low pulse starting at the same instant, about 0.28 µs wide. SCK rises again, and the decoder counts that rising edge as the first clock. SCK then falls together with MOSI about 2.4 µs after CS asserts, and the 32 real clocks follow. Before the scan SCK sat high from the reset edge. Later transactions inspected (the second and the last) start with SCK low and no pulse. The firmware configures SPI1 as Mode 0 (`SPI_POLARITY_LOW`, `SPI_PHASE_1EDGE`). The flash returned a CRC-valid slot 0 regardless. The cause is unknown, and the capture does not show whether the pulse was present at the flash's own CLK pin. A hypothesis, not confirmed in the HAL source, is that the peripheral is enabled by the first transfer. Not checked: whether all 1022 other transactions start with SCK low.

Timing from the capture: CS first fell at about 226.28 ms and last rose at about 351.56 ms on the screenshot axis, a span of about 125.3 ms, against `elapsed_ticks=0x7D` (125 ms). The decoder sample indices (first start 5430616, last end 8437257) give 3,006,641 samples, which equals 125.3 ms only at 24 MS/s. That rate was not recorded, but the two time sources together imply it. Derived from the pasted indices at 24 MS/s: slot 0 to slot 1 CS fall to CS fall is 2944 samples, about 122.7 µs, of which the CS-low part is about 2713 samples, about 113 µs. These are approximate. The wire time at 2.625 MHz for 20 bytes is about 61 µs, so the rest is gaps between bytes and between reads. The capture shows where the time goes, not which C function spends it.

## 5. Predictions against results (689b536)

* Last lines before the stop: predicted `SAMPLE seq=0x000003FF`, `APPEND VERIFIED slot=0x000003FF seq=0x000003FF`, `LOG FULL. WRITES STOPPED.`. Held.
* Boot on the full log: predicted `SCAN_FULL`, `append_slot=0xFFFFFFFF`, `next_seq=0x00000400`, `RECOVERY FULL. WRITES DISABLED.`, no LOGGER STARTED. Held on every field.
* Scan time: predicted 65 to 85 ms (0x41 to 0x55), recorded before the run. Missed. Measured 125 ms (0x7D), matching the extrapolation of about 124 ms from the partial scans (22 ms for 182 reads, 86 ms for 705 reads).
* Last transaction on the wire: predicted MOSI header `03 7F CF F0` and payload starting `FF 03 00 00`. Held.
* First transaction on the wire: predicted a clean `03 7F 90 00` header. Missed as decoded (one extra leading bit); the data was nonetheless the correct record.
* No `06`, `20` or `02` during recovery: held in the decoded capture.

## 6. What this evidence does not cover

* The untrimmed fill logs, the middles of the 169358e and 689b536 runs, the analyzer capture and the full decoder export are lost.
* Slot to address mapping is verified on the wire for slots 0 and 0x3FF only. The check that all 1024 addresses step by exactly 0x10 was not run.
* The CORRUPT skip on real flash with the current code, the `READBACK MISMATCH` line and the SAMPLES SKIPPED path have not fired on hardware.
* No power cycle (USB unplugged) repeat of the full-log boot has been captured, and no cut during a flash program was tested.
* A manual reset interrupted a cycle between the SAMPLE print and the append. That is resume after interruption, not survival of a power cut mid write. Describe torn-record handling as recovered from simulated torn records.
* Datasheet figures (erase times, BME280 measurement time, page program time) and the F446 CRC unit details were not checked against the datasheets.
