First FreeRTOS run, firmware at 80083d8 (PROVISION_LOG 0), 2026-11-21.
Board: NUCLEO-F446RE + BME280 + W25Q64. Raw PuTTY session log.

Sequence: provisioning image flashed and run (log starts there), then the
normal image flashed with PuTTY still open, then left running until the
log was full and samples were being dropped.

What freertos-first-run.log shows:
- Provisioning erased and verified all 4 log sectors (16,384 bytes).
- ~17,000 NUL bytes before the RECOVERY line: UART TX pin held low while
  the ST-Link reflashed the board. Not firmware output.
- RECOVERY status=SCAN_OK append_slot=0 next_seq=0. elapsed_ticks=0
  because the scan stops at the first blank slot (slot 0).
- SAMPLE then APPEND VERIFIED every cycle (SensorTask is higher priority).
- 1024 APPEND VERIFIED lines, slot == seq on every one, no gaps.
- After slot 0x3FF: LOGGER FULL, StorageTask suspends. Seq 0x400-0x403
  enter the queue (depth 4) with no drop. From 0x404 every sample drops.
- Totals: 1548 SAMPLE = 1024 appended + 4 stuck in queue + 520 dropped.
  The drop counter does not include the 4 queued records.
- No SKIPPED, ERROR or EXHAUSTED lines.

Not proven by this log: the 250 ms spacing (no timestamps in this build;
the next build prints the tick in each SAMPLE line), and stack usage.
